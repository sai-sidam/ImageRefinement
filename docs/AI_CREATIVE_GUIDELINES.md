# June & Ember — AI creative guidelines

Ethical principles and prompt guidance for responsible, natural-looking AI image generation. See also [JUNE_EMBER_BRAND.md](JUNE_EMBER_BRAND.md) and [DIRECTOR_BRIEF_AND_VISION.md](DIRECTOR_BRIEF_AND_VISION.md).

---

## I. Introduction & purpose

**Principle:** June & Ember uses AI image generation to support creativity and efficiency while upholding our values of empowerment, authenticity, and inclusivity. This document states our ethical commitments so that AI tools are used responsibly and reflect our brand identity.

---

## II. Core ethical principles for AI image generation

### A. Inclusive representation

We commit to generating diverse and representative imagery that reflects the full spectrum of June & Ember's customer base. This means actively varying demographics (age, ethnicity, body type, cultural background) and avoiding a narrow or homogenous portrayal of women.

### B. Authentic & relatable storytelling

AI-generated images must convey genuine emotion, realistic scenarios, and aspirational yet relatable lifestyles. We prioritize authenticity in expressions, poses, and contexts so our audience connects with the images as truthful extensions of the June & Ember brand experience.

### C. Mitigating harmful stereotypes & bias

We actively guard against perpetuating harmful stereotypes, biases, or unrealistic beauty standards that can be present in AI models. This involves reviewing outputs so that portrayals are positive, empowering, and challenge conventional clichés, fostering a respectful and progressive image of women.

### D. Brand voice & aesthetic consistency

All AI-generated visuals must align with June & Ember's established brand voice, aesthetic, and quality standards. This includes adherence to our brand style, color palettes, lighting preferences, and overall aspirational-yet-approachable feel for a cohesive brand experience.

---

## III. Implementation & oversight

These guidelines inform prompt engineering, output review, and training for everyone using AI image generation. Regular audits should be conducted to ensure ongoing compliance and continuous improvement of our ethical practices.

## IV. Prompting for natural imagery

Use this when writing or tuning prompts so generated images feel like **real photography**, not obviously AI. The tool refines real product photos; the output should keep a natural, lived-in look.

**Why some AI images feel "off":** (1) Too perfect — over-smooth skin, flawless symmetry. (2) Flat or generic lighting — no clear direction or depth. (3) Disconnected from the scene — skin and hair don't react to the same light as the background. (4) Over-corrected color — neutral can look sterile. (5) Plastic/airbrushed look — lack of subtle texture.

**What tends to feel natural:** One clear lighting direction, soft visible shadows; natural skin texture and variation; slight warmth or coolness that fits the scene; subject and background share the same light; subtle asymmetry and lived-in feel. Avoid: flat even light, perfectly smooth skin, over-neutral white balance, "pasted in" look.

**Prompt guidance:** Do ask for: "Natural skin texture," "directional light with soft shadows," "one main light source," "subtle warmth," "lived-in," "authentic," "like real photography." Avoid over-asking for: "Perfect," "flawless," "neutral white balance," "even lighting," "studio-perfect." Preserve the source: "preserve garment color," "improve background and lighting." Prioritize **sharp, clear, high-resolution** and **directional lighting**. For June & Ember: prefer **subtle warmth** and **directional natural light** over maximum neutrality; use "soft natural light with a gentle warmth" or "slightly warm, not overly golden" rather than "neutral" or "no warm cast."

*Source: Expert review (Gemini); implemented per project guidelines.*
