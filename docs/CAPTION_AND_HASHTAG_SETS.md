## Caption Hooks (First Line — ~125 Characters)

**Product / occasion**
- This one goes from brunch to evening without a single change.
- The dress that actually fits the “one piece, three ways” promise.
- We keep coming back to this cut for a reason.
- Your next event? Sorted.
- The piece that gets the most “where’s that from?” in our DMs.

**Style / confidence**
- Confidence isn’t the outfit. But the right outfit doesn’t hurt.
- Some days you need to dress like it’s already a good day.
- The kind of piece you reach for when you want to feel put-together in 10 minutes.
- Style that doesn’t try too hard — that’s the goal.

**Question / engagement**
- Which color would you wear first?
- Save this for your next “I have nothing to wear” moment.
- One piece, three ways — which look is yours?
- What’s your go-to occasion outfit? Drop it below.

**CTA (use as second line or closing)**
- Tap the link to shop.
- Save this for your next event.
- Tag a friend who needs this.
