# Collection and campaign guidelines

How product lines, collections, seasons, and campaigns influence SMM planning and Director briefs. See [JUNE_EMBER_BRAND.md](JUNE_EMBER_BRAND.md) and [SOCIAL_MEDIA_EXPERT.md](SOCIAL_MEDIA_EXPERT.md).

---

## Product / collection specificity

Different product categories can drive different creative choices:

- **Resort collection:** Stories, sets, and lighting can lean into outdoor terraces, beach, golden hour, relaxed elegance.
- **Gala / evening wear:** Mood and sets can be more formal (interior, evening light, statement pose).
- **Day-to-night dresses:** Consider two moments or two slides (day vs evening) or one set that works for both.

The **story** and **set** variables in the Director brief already support this; SMM and Director should align on which collection or product line a post belongs to so the pool of appropriate stories, sets, and mood is clear.

---

## Seasonal and campaign content

- **Seasonal campaigns** (e.g. Summer Soirée, Holiday Glam): Adapt content pillars, stories, sets, and concepts to the season. SMM plans posts with the campaign theme; Director briefs use seasonal sets and lighting where appropriate.
- **Events** (e.g. NYE, Weddings): Briefs can call out event-specific moments and settings.
- **Major launches:** Campaign-specific visuals and concepts live in brand assets; see [BRAND_ASSET_MANAGEMENT.md](BRAND_ASSET_MANAGEMENT.md).

Integrate seasonal and campaign guidance into SMM planning (e.g. a short brief or checklist per campaign) so Director briefs stay aligned with the calendar and messaging.
