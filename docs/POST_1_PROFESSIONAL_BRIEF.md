## Intention for Post 1

- **Role:** First post = first impression. It introduces the brand and sets the visual and strategic standard for the feed.
- **Quality bar:** The image and the content (caption, hashtags, timing) must feel like they came from a professional fashion brand — the same level of care a director and photographer would apply on a real shoot: intentional lighting, coherent environment, real-feeling skin and fabric, no “AI” tells.
- **Outcome:** Viewers should not feel the image is fake or generated; they should feel it belongs in a curated, aspirational fashion feed.

---

## Image quality bar (director + photographer level)

What professionals deliver (from industry practice) and what we must match:

### 1. Lighting

- **One clear primary light source** (e.g. window, doorway, soft daylight from one side) so the scene has direction and depth.
- **Reveal fabric honestly:** Lighting should show the dress’s texture and drape, not flatten or over-dramatize. Soft key light, controlled fill so shadows aren’t muddy, subtle edge so the garment separates from the background.
- **Coherent light:** The model and the environment are lit by the same light — same color temperature, same direction. No “pasted in” look.
- **Subtle warmth** (on-brand): June & Ember = warm neutrals; the image should have a gentle warmth that ties the scene together, not sterile neutral or overly golden.

### 2. Environment and set

- **Story and set align:** The location supports the story (“June & Ember is here” — arrival, ease, somewhere special). Elegant interior or resort feel: corridor, lobby, or light-filled room; warm neutrals; clean, uncluttered.
- **Specific, not generic:** The space should feel like a real place (materials, architecture, light direction), not a vague “nice room.”

### 3. Model and pose

- **Natural:** Relaxed, confident pose; natural hair (soft movement or natural fall); natural expression and body language — like a real fashion shoot, not stiff or over-posed.
- **Face:** Hero shot = with face; natural, confident expression.
- **Skin:** Retain subtle texture and variation; no plastic or airbrushed smoothness. Sharp and clear, but not “perfect” in a way that reads as AI.

### 4. Garment and color

- **Dress unchanged:** Backless Wide Strap Maxi Dress — exact color, pattern, and design preserved. No color shift.
- **Color accuracy and coherence:** Garment colors true; overall scene has unified tonal balance and a consistent color temperature (warm, on-brand).

### 5. Technical

- **Sharp, high-resolution:** Professional fashion-photography quality; fabric detail visible; no soft or muddy downgrade.
- **Composition:** Product as hero, center frame; subject in safe zone (center 70%; avoid critical detail in edges for grid crop).
- **Format:** 4:5 (1080 × 1350 px) for Instagram feed.

### 6. What we avoid (no gimmicks, no AI tells)

- No flat, even lighting with no direction.
- No over-smooth or plastic skin.
- No subject lit differently from the background.
- No over-neutral or sterile color.
- No “perfect” symmetry or flawless execution that feels synthetic.
- No busy or cluttered set; no cold or casual environments that don’t match the brand.

- **Caption:** Short, welcoming, on-brand (“June & Ember is here…”). Clear CTA (e.g. tap link to shop).
- **Hashtags:** Full set per ALL_POSTS_READY (Set A); consistent with strategy.
- **Timing:** Post in the chosen window (e.g. Tue–Thu 10 AM–1 PM or 6–8 PM) for consistency.
- **Profile:** Business/Creator account, bio + link to junenember.com before posting.

The **content** (caption, hashtags, CTA) and **strategy** (why this is the first post, what it says about the brand) are part of the same quality bar as the image — everything on par.

## How to get to this quality

1. **Reference-image path (recommended):** Save a **real photo** from a professional fashion brand (e.g. talbotsofficial, astee_official) that already meets the bar above. Run the tool with `--reference-path=./that-photo.jpg` (and our product image). The output = that exact frame with our dress and a new face. That is the most reliable way to get director-level quality, because the reference was shot by a real crew.
2. **Prompt path:** Use the **Post 1 pro-grade prompt** (see `instagram-output/prompts/post-01-pro-grade-prompt.txt`) which encodes the criteria above. Generate a variant and compare to the reference path or to existing variants.
3. **Choose:** Pick the image that best meets the brief above — then publish with the caption and hashtags from ALL_POSTS_READY.
