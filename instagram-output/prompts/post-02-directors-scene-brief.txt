Director's scene for Post 2 — same quality bar as Post 1, different set.

POST 2 PRODUCT: Checkered Jumpsuit. Caption angle: "This one goes from brunch to evening without a single change."

SET (not the same as Post 1): Resort or hotel lobby / lounge — a transitional, elegant space that fits "brunch to evening."
- Location: Light-filled lobby, lounge, or atrium area. Elegant interior: warm neutrals, clean lines. Could include a low sofa, armchair, or bench; subtle greenery or one architectural detail (arch, column); warm wall or soft texture. Real place, not generic.
- No mirror/platform/hangers (those were Post 1). This set says "I'm here, I'm ready — same outfit, different moment."
- Model placement (critical): She must have a natural reason to be where she is. E.g. seated on the sofa or armchair (in the furniture, not standing in front of it); or standing by the window or archway as if she just walked in or is pausing. Never standing awkwardly in front of a couch or in the middle of the room with no relation to the space. She belongs in the scene — sitting, leaning, or pausing by the light.
- Lighting: Soft, even, professional. Same light on the jumpsuit and the environment — one coherent world. Natural, balanced color temperature. The walls and setting are already warm neutrals; do not over-warm the image or add a golden/amber tint. No harsh shadows.
- Mood: "From brunch to evening in one piece" — confident, at ease in the space. The jumpsuit and the environment belong together; the story is versatility and ease.

Model: Natural pose, face visible (hero shot). Hair and expression coherent with the scene. Full-length so the jumpsuit is shown as designed. June & Ember aesthetic throughout.
